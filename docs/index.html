What is live?

BTC price quotes are requested about every 15 seconds while this page is open, if the public exchanges allow the browser connection. Saved paper results and radar reports are checked about every minute. The top-wallet research report is checked every five minutes. GitHub's paper simulation itself is scheduled hourly and may be delayed.

Nothing on this page places trades. Quotes can be delayed or unavailable. The HTML is a public, read-only display. No exchange credentials or wallet keys belong here.
